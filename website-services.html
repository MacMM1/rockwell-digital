FAQ

Website Services, answered.

Should I redesign my site or rebuild it from scratch?

We'll audit what you've got first and tell you honestly which one you actually need. Sometimes a redesign is overkill; sometimes patching an old site costs more than starting fresh.

What do you actually build with?

Hand-built HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no bloated page-builder plugins slowing your site down. It loads faster and is easier to maintain long-term.

Do you handle hosting?

Yes, if you want it — or we hand you the files and access to manage it yourself. Either way, it's registered in your name, not ours.

What if I need changes after launch?

Ongoing management is available month to month, only if you want it. There's no obligation to sign up for maintenance you don't need.
