FAQ

Google Business Profile & Local SEO, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes, for our first founding clients — no catch, no obligation to buy anything else afterwards. All we ask in return is a short testimonial once you're happy with the result.

I already have a Google Business Profile — can you still help?

Yes. Most existing profiles are incomplete or have inconsistent details somewhere. We'll check yours, fix what's missing or wrong, and tell you honestly if it was already in good shape.

How is this different from the free website audit?

The audit looks at your website. This looks at how your business shows up on Google Maps and in local search results — a different, and often faster, place to start if you don't have a website yet at all.

Do I need a website for this to work?

No. A Google Business Profile works on its own. It becomes even more useful once you have a site to link it to, but it's not a requirement to get started.
