FAQ

Ads Management, answered.

I don't have a Google or Meta ads account — is that a problem?

Not at all. We'll walk you through setting one up on your first call, using your own business email and card. You own the account from the moment it exists — we're just granted access to manage it.

How long before I see results?

Ad platforms need real time to learn. Most accounts need several weeks before results are meaningful to judge — that's why we ask for a short minimum term, so a campaign has a fair chance to actually work.

What's the minimum ad spend?

We'll recommend a realistic minimum for your industry and goals during onboarding — enough to actually gather data, not just enough to look busy.

Which platforms do you manage?

Google Search and Local Services Ads, plus Meta (Facebook & Instagram). Local Services Ads are pay-per-lead rather than pay-per-click, and often an easier way in for local trades.

Do you require a long contract?

A short initial term, then month to month — long enough to judge results fairly, no longer.
